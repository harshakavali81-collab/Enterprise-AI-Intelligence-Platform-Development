# Security and privacy scope

All fixtures are synthetic. No stored user inputs. No automatic fetching of suspicious links. Public-health content is not diagnostic. Emergency and flood output cannot create official warnings or dispatch. Missing-person matching uses pseudonymous record IDs, similarity suggestions only, no photos/face recognition and never merges identities. All administrative and municipal actions require external real authentication and authorized human review; these are **not included**.

Known gaps: rate limiting, authenticated staff roles, durable audit log, encryption at rest, formal data-retention system, PII redaction, licensed source ingestion, operational rollback, monitoring, pen test and clinical/disaster domain sign-off. Do not expose the demo endpoint to untrusted traffic until these are addressed.

# 04. Enterprise RAG Architecture & Citations

![RAG Workflow](../diagrams/rag_workflow.png)

## Overview
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is the foundational architecture ensuring that conversational AI responses are grounded exclusively in verified corporate documents rather than speculative parametric memory.

---

## ❓ Why RAG for the Enterprise?
1. **Zero Hallucination Guarantee**: Standard LLMs hallucinate policies when asked company-specific questions (such as travel allowances or sick leave limits). RAG constrains the model's response to retrieved source passages.
2. **Access Control & Clearance**: Different employees have different security clearances. An employee should not access executive severance terms or acquisition filings. RAG enforces document-level and chunk-level RBAC filtering before retrieval.
3. **Traceability & Audit Readiness**: Enterprise legal and compliance teams require audit-ready proof. Every answer generated by our RAG system includes verifiable citations down to the document name, section, and page number.
4. **Dynamic Knowledge Updates**: When company policies update, documents are re-indexed instantly into `pgvector` without requiring expensive, time-consuming model fine-tuning.

---

## ⚙️ The RAG Ingestion & Chunking Pipeline

```
DOCUMENT (PDF / DOCX / TXT / XLSX)
   │
   ▼
[ File Validation & Format Parsing ]
   │
   ▼
[ Text Sanitization & Cleaning ]
   │
   ▼
[ Semantic Sliding Chunking ] (500 chars, 80-char overlap, section-aware)
   │
   ▼
[ Metadata Enrichment ] (doc_id, title, department, access_level, page_number)
   │
   ▼
[ Dense Vector Embedding ] (768-dimensional normalized vector)
   │
   ▼
[ PostgreSQL + pgvector Storage ] (HNSW Indexing)
```

### Semantic Chunking Strategy
- **Chunk Size**: 500 characters (~100–125 tokens), optimal for preserving localized factual statements without diluting semantic density.
- **Overlap**: 80 characters to ensure context continuity across chunk boundaries.
- **Header Awareness**: Chunks retain enclosing markdown headings (`#`, `##`) to maintain contextual hierarchy.
- **Page Binding**: Every chunk explicitly records physical page numbers for exact citation tracing.

---

## 🔍 Hybrid Retrieval Engine
Pure vector search often misses exact numeric values, policy numbers, or acronyms. Pure keyword search fails on semantic paraphrasing. Our platform uses a weighted **Hybrid Retrieval Engine**:

$$\text{Hybrid Score} = \alpha \cdot \text{Dense Vector Score} + (1 - \alpha) \cdot \text{BM25 Lexical Score}$$

- **Dense Vector Similarity**: Cosine distance over 768-dimensional embeddings ($\alpha = 0.65$).
- **BM25 Lexical Matching**: Exact token and n-gram overlap ($1 - \alpha = 0.35$).
- **RBAC Clearance Filter**: Chunks requiring higher clearance than the user's role are eliminated before scoring.

---

## 🎯 Contextual Reranking
The top 6 retrieved chunks undergo cross-encoder contextual reranking:
- **Title Relevance**: Boosts chunks whose document title directly matches query entities (+0.15).
- **Numeric & Monetary Query Boost**: Boosts chunks containing explicit currency markers (`₹`, `limit`, `days`, `%`) when numeric bounds are queried (+0.20).
- **Consecutive N-Gram Overlap**: Rewards exact multi-word phrase matches (+0.10).
